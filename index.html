PROJECT LA

Poems set to music.

An author's music project founded in Yerevan in 2015 by Levon Arevshatyan, the LA of the name. Every song is his own, written to the verse of Teryan, Charents and Sevak, of Poe, Byron and Dickinson and many other poets.

The project

One composer, many voices

Levon Arevshatyan is an economist by profession. The repertoire of PROJECT LA consists only of his songs. It began with English-language poetry set to rock, and later moved through reggae, romance and urban folk.

The first song was A Dream Within a Dream, on the poem by Edgar Allan Poe. The first song in Armenian came from Vahan Teryan's Կարոտ (Longing).

Most songs are sung by Gor Sujyan. Producer Suren Sahakyan is also the artistic director, and directs the concerts, their light and sound, and the videos.

Since 2015

The story so far

  1. 2015

    PROJECT LA is founded in Yerevan. The first concert, on 3 December at the Yans club, is a closed evening for friends.

  2. 2016

    The first album, Imitation, comes out with eight songs in English. A second closed concert follows on 7 June at the Karen Demirchyan Sports and Concert Complex.

  3. 2017

    The first album in Armenian: four songs on poems by Vahan Teryan, «Իրիկնաժամ», «Երբ կյանքը սուր փշերով», «Ցանկություն» and «Կարոտ».

  4. 2019

    The first public concert, on 15 June at the Karen Demirchyan Complex, sells out ten days ahead. Who Shall I Meet, on the poem by Charents, is named Song of the Year.

  5. 2020

    One new album and eleven music videos.

  6. 2021

    Requiem, with words by Avet Barseghyan, is released in memory of those who fell in the forty-four-day war. On 5 June the band plays Gyumri, its first concert outside Yerevan; tickets go as soon as it is announced.

  7. 2022

    In My Dreams is named Song of the Year.

  8. 2023

    Marionette is named Song of the Year. On 15 December the band plays the Alex Theatre in Glendale, USA; the concert sells out two weeks ahead.

  9. 2024

    The Story is named Song of the Year.

  10. 2025

    The Armenian Music Academy names PROJECT LA Best Group-Project of the Year.

  11. 2026

    On 3 October the band plays Arev Arena in Yerevan; the concert sells out a week ahead.
